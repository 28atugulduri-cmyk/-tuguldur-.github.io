Basketball sneakers history

From the 70s to today, evolution of performance and culture

70s-Classics

High-tops rise. Sneakers move from the gym to the streets.

80s-Icons emerge

Bold colors, classic silhouettes. Michael Jordan changes the game with Air Jordans.

90s-Global takeover

Superstars and signature shoes dominate. Sneakers become status symbols worldwide.

2000s-Tech & style

Advanced cushioning, lightweight materials, and flashy designs take over the courts.

2010s-Analytics & peformance

Shoes optimized for speed, agility, and precise movements.

2020s-Future hype

Next-gen tech, collaborations, limited drops. Sneakers now a cultural phenomenon.

This site is build as a DoFE project. It breaks down the history of basketball shoes and the players, technology, and moments that shaped them.
