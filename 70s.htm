1970s - Birth of Basketball Sneakers

The decade where basketball shoes became part of culture.

Converse Dominance

Converse Chuck Taylors were still the most common basketball shoes during the early 70s.

Rise of High Tops

High-top sneakers became popular for ankle support and quickly became the basketball standard.

Sneakers Leave the Court

Basketball shoes slowly entered street fashion and youth culture outside sports.

The 1970s were the foundation years of basketball sneakers. Most players still wore simple canvas shoes, but companies started experimenting with better support and durability. High-top silhouettes became more common because players wanted extra ankle protection during fast and physical games. Converse dominated the courts, while brands like Adidas slowly entered basketball culture. During this decade, sneakers also started appearing outside gyms and became part of everyday street fashion.
